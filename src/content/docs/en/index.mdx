## Welcome to RTM Wiki
RTM Wiki is a site that compiles information about playing and developing add-ons for RealTrainMod (RTM).

## What is RealTrainMod?
RealTrainMod (hereafter referred to as RTM) is a railway mod for Minecraft: Java Edition,  
developed by the Japanese creator **ngt5479**.  
For more detailed information about RTM, please refer to [Basic Information and Appeal of RealTrainMod](usage/basic).

## Celebrating the 12th Anniversary of RealTrainMod
RTM celebrated its 12th anniversary on July 13, 2025.  
We would like to take this opportunity to extend our congratulations.
